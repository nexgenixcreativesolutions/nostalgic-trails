Frequently Asked
Do I need to pose for the photos?
Not at all — that's the point. We want you to forget about cameras entirely. Think of your session as a day trip where you can run, dance, laugh, and just be yourself. We document what's real.
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend at least 3–6 months for weddings and debuts, and 3–4 weeks for portrait sessions. A 30% reservation fee secures your date.
Do you travel for shoots?
Yes — we cover the Philippines and accept destination bookings. Travel fees apply depending on location; reach out for a custom quote.
When will I receive my photos?
Delivery timelines depend on your package. We'll discuss this during our initial consultation. We also offer rush delivery options — just ask.
Can packages be customized?
Absolutely. Packages are a starting point. We're happy to build something tailored to your day and budget — just send us a message.

Ready to Reserve Your Date?

Dates book up fast. Send us a message and we'll confirm availability within 24 hours.
