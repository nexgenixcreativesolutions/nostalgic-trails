Frequently Asked
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 3–6 months in advance for weddings, and 4–6 weeks for portrait sessions. Peak season (November–February) fills quickly.
Do you travel for destination shoots?
Absolutely. We cover all of the Philippines and accept destination bookings internationally. Travel fees apply depending on location — ask us for a custom quote.
When will I receive my photos?
Standard delivery is 4–6 weeks. Rush delivery (within 7 days) is available as an add-on. You'll receive your gallery via private online link.
Is a deposit required to secure the date?
Yes — a 30% non-refundable reservation fee is required to hold your date. The remaining balance is due two weeks before your event.

Of course. Fill out the form below and tell us exactly what you need. We're happy to build something tailored to your event and budget.
